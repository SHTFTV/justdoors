# 24-Hour Emergency Calls: Hoarding and Temporary Enclosures

**Emergency hoarding and board-up work address different temporary needs. Explain whether you need a damaged opening covered, an area separated from occupants or a temporary enclosure while permanent work is arranged.**

## Distinguish the temporary scope

Board-up usually concerns a damaged door, window or similar opening. A hoarding enquiry may concern a larger area, work zone or temporary barrier. Send a wide view that shows the surrounding space as well as the affected opening. State whether the purpose is security, weather protection, privacy or separating work from everyday activity; the proposed arrangement needs a site-specific assessment.

## Explain who still needs access

Identify occupied areas, public routes, deliveries and the site contact. The temporary arrangement must be planned with the responsible site team so necessary access and building operations are addressed. Do not assume that covering an opening is appropriate without understanding how it is used.

## Plan beyond the first visit

Give the expected duration, any planned permanent replacement and the party responsible for follow-up. Ask the quotation to identify installation, adjustments, removal and making good. Temporary works should have a clear handover contact and a route for reporting damage or changed conditions.
