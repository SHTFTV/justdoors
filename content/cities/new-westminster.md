# Door Supply & Installation in New Westminster, BC

**Just Doors supports door supply and installation coordination for New Westminster homes, heritage renovations, strata buildings, rental properties and commercial spaces.**

## Existing-building door work
Renovations often require careful field verification because existing walls, frames and openings may differ from the drawings. Photos, measurements and access to the opening help distinguish a direct replacement from work requiring frame or wall adjustment.

## Multi-family and rated openings
Suite entries, corridors, stairs and service rooms may have specific fire, smoke, acoustic, accessibility or hardware requirements. Those requirements must be taken from the approved project information and applicable listed assembly.
