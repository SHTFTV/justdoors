## Fire-rated and fireproof doors in Langley

Under the BC Building Code, Langley townhouse and single-family garage-to-house doors require a fire-rated, self-closing assembly — one of the most common and most-inspected openings on a Willoughby project; multi-family suite entries carry 20- to 45-minute ratings with self-closing and positive-latching hardware and smoke seals; and stairwells, shafts, and high-hazard rooms escalate to 90-minute, 1-hour, and 2-hour assemblies. A fire door is a tested assembly — the leaf label, frame label, hinges, closer, and latching hardware all have to match a listed configuration, and NFPA 80 governs the field conditions: the perimeter gap, the maximum undercut, positive latching, and no field modifications outside the listing. Langley's fire services check these. Just Doors supplies fire assemblies as coordinated, labelled packages with the documentation inspectors look for — and on high-volume townhouse work, gets the rated garage-to-house door right on every unit.

## Langley door FAQ

**Is my project under the City or the Township of Langley?**
Langley is two municipalities. The Township of Langley covers Willoughby, Walnut Grove, Brookswood, Fort Langley, Aldergrove, and the agricultural lands; the City of Langley is the compact central urban core. **Do townhouse garage-to-house doors need to be fire-rated in Langley?**
Yes. The BC Building Code requires the door between an attached garage and the living space to be a fire-rated, self-closing, tightly sealed assembly. On Willoughby townhouse projects it's on nearly every unit and is a frequent inspection item, so it needs to be a listed assembly, not a standard interior door.
