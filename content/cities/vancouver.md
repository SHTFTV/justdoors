## Vancouver jurisdiction matters

The City of Vancouver administers the Vancouver Building By-law rather than simply using the provincial BC Building Code as the local building regulation. That makes it important to confirm the current by-law, permit conditions, design documents, fire/life-safety requirements and product listings for each project.

Just Doors can help organize the door schedule and identify information that needs confirmation, but the project architect, code consultant, authority having jurisdiction, manufacturer listings and approved construction documents remain the controlling sources for code compliance.

## Fire-rated doors

Fire-rated openings are assemblies, not just door leaves. The door, frame, glazing, hardware, closer, latch, seals, field modifications and installation conditions may all affect the listed assembly. Required ratings vary by building, occupancy, separation and opening location.

For that reason, Just Doors does not advertise a single rating as correct for all Vancouver suite entries, stairs, service rooms or corridors. We coordinate the requested assembly against the project documents and applicable listings before supply.

## Acoustic, security and access-control openings

Acoustic and security performance depends on the complete opening. Door construction, seals, thresholds, frames and hardware need to be selected for the project's stated requirements. Electrified hardware and access control also require coordination with the electrical, low-voltage, security and life-safety design.

We can supply and coordinate these components, but we do not represent a generic configuration as automatically compliant or suitable for every project.
