Security doors and access control across Coquitlam

Security spans every Coquitlam sector. For residential and townhouse clients — including the many new Burke Mountain homes — reinforced entry doors with multi-point locking, solid or steel-reinforced cores, and strike reinforcement. For multi-family towers at the SkyTrain hubs, a system: secured main-entry doors with electronic access control, parkade and bike-room doors on fobs, amenity access tied to the building system, and suite entries that resist forced entry while meeting egress and fire code. For commercial tenancies, storefront security, secured back-of-house access, and after-hours lockdown hardware.

Security hardware has to coexist with life-safety code: a secured stairwell or exit door still has to allow free egress under the BC Building Code and NFPA 101, so electrified hardware, delayed-egress devices, and access-controlled openings must satisfy both the security goal and the egress requirement at once. Electronic access changes the door and frame prep before hardware arrives — power transfer, strike or mag-lock prep, and low-voltage coordination, all resolved at the door schedule. Just Doors specifies security and access-controlled doors as complete, code-satisfying assemblies.

Fire-rated and fireproof doors in Coquitlam

Under the BC Building Code, Coquitlam multi-family suite entries carry 20- to 45-minute ratings with self-closing and positive-latching hardware and smoke seals; townhouse and single-family garage-to-house doors require a fire-rated, self-closing assembly; and stairwells, shafts, and high-hazard rooms escalate to 90-minute, 1-hour, and 2-hour assemblies. On Burke Mountain's forest-interface homes, the garage-to-house separation and any wildfire-related assembly requirements deserve particular attention. A fire door is a tested assembly — the leaf label, frame label, hinges, closer, and latching hardware all have to match a listed configuration, and NFPA 80 governs the field conditions. Coquitlam Fire/Rescue checks these. Just Doors supplies fire assemblies as coordinated, labelled packages with the documentation inspectors look for.

Acoustic doors for Coquitlam's tower density

The Coquitlam Centre and Burquitlam towers stack hundreds of suites, and acoustic performance is an expectation — doors are the weak point in an otherwise isolated suite. Just Doors specifies acoustic suite entries as a system — a solid core selected for its STC contribution, full perimeter gasketing, and an automatic drop seal at the sill — so the assembled opening stays close to the surrounding wall's rating. In townhouse and low-rise work, the same discipline applies at demising and shared-corridor doors.

Climate, terrain, and door performance in Coquitlam

Coquitlam shares the Lower Mainland's wet climate, and buildings under construction — before HVAC is commissioned — move through humidity swings that can swell and bind wood doors, so doors should be acclimatized to the building's eventual moisture content before hanging, with moisture-stable cores and sealed edges. Burke Mountain's elevation and forest exposure add weather demands on exterior openings. Just Doors manages these conditions as part of the door scope rather than discovering them at handover.

Coquitlam door FAQ

Does Coquitlam follow the same door code as Vancouver? No. Coquitlam follows the British Columbia Building Code, administered by the City of Coquitlam's building permits department with fire review through Coquitlam Fire/Rescue, whereas the City of Vancouver uses its own standalone bylaw. Assemblies should be verified against the BC Building Code and Coquitlam's requirements.

Can Just Doors standardize a door package across a Burke Mountain subdivision? Yes — and it's where we add the most value in Coquitlam's high-volume new-home areas. We specify the door package once against the home and unit plans, then repeat it consistently across every unit: the same hardware groups, ratings, and finishes, with the fire-rated garage-to-house door handled correctly on every home.

What fire rating do Coquitlam multi-family suite entries need? Typically a certified 20- to 45-minute fire-protection rating with self-closing hardware, positive latching, and smoke seals; stairwells, shafts, and high-hazard rooms escalate higher. The full assembly must match a listed configuration to pass Coquitlam Fire/Rescue review.

Do Burke Mountain forest-interface homes need special door treatment? The fire-rated garage-to-house separation is required as on any home, and any wildfire-interface assembly requirements should be reviewed. Durable, weather-appropriate exterior assemblies suit the hillside forest exposure.

Do you handle towers at Coquitlam Centre and Burquitlam? Yes. We supply and schedule full tower door packages — suite entries, stairwell and shaft doors, amenity, parkade, and service-room openings — standardized across multi-tower, multi-phase developments at the SkyTrain hubs.
