Two jurisdictions: City and Township of Langley

Langley is two municipalities. The Township of Langley covers the large, fast-growing area — Willoughby, Walnut Grove, Brookswood, Fort Langley, Aldergrove, and the agricultural lands — while the City of Langley is the compact urban core at the centre, where the SkyTrain extension is set to terminate. Both follow the British Columbia Building Code, the Township administered by the Township of Langley's building department and the City by the City of Langley's, with their own fire services. Security doors and access control across Langley

Security spans every Langley sector. For residential and townhouse clients — the bulk of Langley's growth — reinforced entry doors with multi-point locking, solid or steel-reinforced cores, and strike reinforcement. For multi-family buildings in the City core, a system: secured main-entry doors with electronic access control, parkade and bike-room doors on fobs, amenity access tied to the building system, and suite entries that resist forced entry while meeting egress and fire code. Security hardware has to coexist with life-safety code: a secured stairwell or exit door still has to allow free egress under the BC Building Code and NFPA 101, so electrified hardware, delayed-egress devices, and access-controlled openings must satisfy both the security goal and the egress requirement at once. Electronic access changes the door and frame prep before hardware arrives — power transfer, strike or mag-lock prep, and low-voltage coordination, all resolved at the door schedule. Just Doors specifies security and access-controlled doors as complete, code-satisfying assemblies.

Fire-rated and fireproof doors in Langley

Under the BC Building Code, Langley townhouse and single-family garage-to-house doors require a fire-rated, self-closing assembly — one of the most common and most-inspected openings on a Willoughby project; multi-family suite entries carry 20- to 45-minute ratings with self-closing and positive-latching hardware and smoke seals; and stairwells, shafts, and high-hazard rooms escalate to 90-minute, 1-hour, and 2-hour assemblies. A fire door is a tested assembly — the leaf label, frame label, hinges, closer, and latching hardware all have to match a listed configuration, and NFPA 80 governs the field conditions: the perimeter gap, the maximum undercut, positive latching, and no field modifications outside the listing. Langley's fire services check these. Just Doors supplies fire assemblies as coordinated, labelled packages with the documentation inspectors look for — and on high-volume townhouse work, gets the rated garage-to-house door right on every unit.

Acoustic doors for Langley's density

As Langley's townhouse and multi-family density climbs, acoustic performance moves from a nicety to an expectation, and doors are the weak point in an otherwise isolated unit or suite. Just Doors specifies acoustic suite and unit entries as a system — a solid core selected for its STC contribution, full perimeter gasketing, and an automatic drop seal at the sill — so the assembled opening stays close to the surrounding wall's rating. In townhouse work, the same discipline applies at demising-wall and shared-corridor doors, keeping sound where it belongs between units.

Climate and door performance in Langley

Langley sits in the wetter, more variable Fraser Valley, and buildings under construction — before HVAC is commissioned — move through humidity swings that can swell and bind wood doors, so doors should be acclimatized to the building's eventual moisture content before hanging, with moisture-stable cores and sealed edges on exterior-adjacent openings. On a fast Willoughby schedule, this is the difference between doors that operate cleanly at handover and a callback list of binding leaves in the first wet season.

Langley door FAQ

Is my project under the City or the Township of Langley? Langley is two municipalities. The Township of Langley covers Willoughby, Walnut Grove, Brookswood, Fort Langley, Aldergrove, and the agricultural lands; the City of Langley is the compact central urban core. Both follow the BC Building Code but are administered by their own building departments and fire services — we scope your project to the correct authority.

Do townhouse garage-to-house doors need to be fire-rated in Langley? Yes. The BC Building Code requires the door between an attached garage and the living space to be a fire-rated, self-closing, tightly sealed assembly. On Willoughby townhouse projects it's on nearly every unit and is a frequent inspection item, so it needs to be a listed assembly, not a standard interior door.

Can Just Doors standardize a door package across a large Willoughby townhouse complex? Yes — it's where we add the most value in Langley. We specify the door package once against your unit plans, then repeat it consistently across every unit and building: the same hardware groups, ratings, and finishes, with the rated garage-to-house door handled correctly on every unit. That standardization compresses procurement and inspection risk across the whole development.

What fire rating do Langley multi-family suite entries need? Typically a certified 20- to 45-minute fire-protection rating with self-closing hardware, positive latching, and smoke seals; stairwells, shafts, and high-hazard rooms escalate higher. The full assembly must match a listed configuration to pass fire review.

Do you handle industrial and agricultural door work in Aldergrove and Gloucester? Yes. We supply hollow-metal commercial and industrial man-doors, warehouse and rated-separation openings, and durable exterior assemblies suited to Langley's industrial estates and agricultural buildings.
