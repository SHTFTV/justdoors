Door Planning Guides

Practical checklists for preparing a door package, assessing an existing opening and comparing installation quotes.

How to Compare Commercial Door Quotes Without Missing Scope

Compare commercial door quotes against one opening schedule and one set of inclusions. Product differences, excluded hardware, delivery assumptions and unfinished surrounding work can make similar totals describe very different projects.

What to Include in a Door Schedule Before Requesting a Quote

A useful door quote starts with a clear opening list. Give every opening an identifier, describe the intended assembly and separate confirmed requirements from questions that still need a project decision.

Emergency Board-Up: Information to Have Ready When You Call

When a door or window is damaged, a clear description helps coordinate temporary protection and the later permanent repair. Provide the address, a safe description of the opening and a reachable site contact before attempting to gather detailed measurements.

Pressed-Steel Door Replacement: What to Check Before Ordering

A pressed-steel door quote needs more than a width and height. Document the existing frame, hardware preparations, exposure and intended use so the replacement package can be reviewed as a complete opening.

Replace the Door Only, or the Door and Frame?

The right replacement scope depends on the existing opening, the new door’s requirements and the work needed around it. Check the frame, hardware interfaces and surrounding finishes before pricing a leaf-only replacement.
